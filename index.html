01 / ABOUT

I turn difficult chemistry into controlled, reproducible experiments.

My research background spans highly reactive molecular systems where careful planning, rigorous technique, and disciplined characterization are essential. I have worked with low-valent aluminum species, actinide precursors, redox-active metalloligands, and polyoxometalate-based chemistry.

I am especially interested in R&D environments where fundamental chemistry supports practical advances in materials, semiconductor technologies, catalysis, energy, specialty chemicals, or advanced manufacturing.
